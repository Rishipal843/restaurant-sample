About White Rock Bistro

Located along Marine Drive in White Rock, BC, White Rock Bistro offers an exceptional blend of West Coast fine dining, fresh Pacific catches, and flame-seared AAA steaks.

Whether you are joining us for a family dinner or an intimate candlelight date by the window, every detail—from our warm ambient lighting to our hand-selected local wines—is designed for an unforgettable dining experience overlooking the water.
