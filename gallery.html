Our Spaces & Details

Stories From Our Dining Room

01 / ELEGANT DESIGN

Royal Navy & Crisp Off-White Elegance

Every corner of White Rock Bistro is designed to combine airy openness with regal sophistication. Our signature chair dressings with deep blue sashes mirror the surrounding Pacific ocean waves right outside Marine Drive.

Spacious seating designed for large gatherings and family dinners.
02 / NATURAL HARMONY

Coastal Views Meets Fresh Greenery

Large grid windows bathe the dining area in soft natural light while hanging plants and floral arrangements bring the outside in. Enjoy peaceful afternoon dining with unhindered street and oceanfront views.

Hand-arranged floral centerpieces on glass reflection tables.
03 / BRAND HERITAGE

The White Rock Bistro Experience

From the moment you walk past our signature wall, you're greeted with warmth and attentive service. Plush green velvet table covers and polished oak seating ensure your time with us feels luxurious yet comforting.

A welcoming environment where memorable conversations unfold.
04 / ROMANTIC EVENINGS

Intimate Candlelight Dinners

As dusk settles over White Rock, our dining room transforms. Soft candle glows reflect off wine glasses and raindrops on the glass panes, creating the perfect backdrop for romantic anniversaries and special celebrations.

Soft background music paired with curated wine selections.
05 / PERSPECTIVE & SPACE

Thoughtful Table Layouts

We prioritize comfort and privacy. Whether you're booking a table for two by the window or reserving a central long table for friends, every seating arrangement offers ample room and relaxing acoustics.

Designed to comfortably host intimate dates or group parties.
06 / EVENING MAGIC

Rainy West Coast Nights

There's a unique magic to dining by the waterfront during rainy British Columbia evenings. Warm candlelight inside creates a cozy sanctuary while you enjoy hot comfort food and premium wines.

Open until 11:00 PM for late-night food and wine lovers.
